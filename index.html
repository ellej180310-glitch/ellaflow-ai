Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about websites, AI automation, project timelines, support, and future upgrades.

How long does a project take?

+

Simple websites may take a few days to two weeks. Larger projects with AI, automation, dashboards, or custom systems may take longer depending on complexity.

Can AI be added to an existing website?

+

Yes. AI assistants, contact automation, customer support tools, and workflow features can be integrated into existing websites depending on the platform.

Do you offer support after launch?

+

Yes. Support can include testing, updates, troubleshooting, email setup, hosting guidance, and future feature improvements.

Can I upgrade later?

+

Yes. A business can start with a basic website and later add automation, AI chat, payments, dashboards, databases, and customer portals.

Is payment built into this website?

+

The site is ready for payment integration. A secure provider like Stripe should be used so card numbers, CVV, and billing details are handled safely.

What makes EllaFlow AI different?

+

EllaFlow AI focuses on practical business tools: websites, automation, AI support, lead capture, customer communication, and scalable digital workflows.
